# Refund Policy

## Standard Refunds
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Partial Refunds
After the 30-day window, partial refunds of up to 50% may be issued at the discretion of the support team. Partial refunds take 10-14 business days to process.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Digital downloads that have been fully accessed
- Subscription fees for partially used billing periods
- Custom or personalized orders

## How to Request a Refund
1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to Order History
3. Select the order you wish to refund
4. Click "Request Refund" and fill in the reason
5. Our team will review within 2 business days
