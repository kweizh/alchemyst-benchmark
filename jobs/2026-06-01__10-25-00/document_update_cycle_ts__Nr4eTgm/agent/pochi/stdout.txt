The supported way to refresh content is the **Delete-Then-Add** update cycle. See https://getalchemystai.com/docs/advanced/troubleshooting and the TypeScript Quickstart at https://getalchemystai.com/docs/getting-started/quickstart. You will build a small Node.js + TypeScript CLI that exercises this full update cycle end-to-end against the real Alchemyst AI service using the official TypeScript SDK (`@alchemystai/sdk`).
